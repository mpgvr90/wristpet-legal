Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who we are

WRISTPET is a walking companion for Apple Watch and iPhone. It is published by Tetiana Lahotska ("we", "us"). Contact: codexxdev77@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the app handles, where it stays and what choices you have.

In short

  • Everything the app uses stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We never receive it.
  • There is no account, no registration and no server of ours. The app makes no network requests of its own.
  • Apple Health data is read only with your permission, is never written back, and is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.
  • WRISTPET is a one-time paid download from the App Store. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. We do not sell data.

Apple Health

With your permission, WRISTPET reads from Apple Health:

  • today's step count and number of workouts;
  • daily step totals for the previous 14 complete days, at most once a day, for the weekly rhythm and an optional goal suggestion.

When new steps are recorded, the app may also read today's total in the background, about once an hour at most, so your watch face and widgets stay current without opening the app.

WRISTPET reads only daily totals, not individual samples, heart rate or location, and it does not write anything to Apple Health. If you do not grant access, you can still care for your companion; automatic step progress is simply unavailable. The optional walk timer never adds steps.

Data stored only on your devices

The app stores on your devices: your companion and its progress, goals, care moments, settings, walk memories (date, duration, an optional feeling and the walk idea), story postcards and up to 35 days of daily step totals.

Widgets and watch complications read this data on the same device. If you use both an iPhone and an Apple Watch, the two devices exchange it directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity; it does not pass through any server of ours.

Story "routes" are imagined. The app does not use your location.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules one local notification on your device when a walk timer ends. Permission is asked the first time you start a walk. The notification is created on the device and is not sent through any server.

Payment: the App Store

WRISTPET is bought once through the Apple App Store under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card details, your name or your Apple Account email; we only see the aggregated sales reports Apple provides to developers.

Permissions

  • Apple Health - read-only access to steps and workouts, requested when you tap "Enable step access".
  • Notifications - for the end of a walk, requested when you start your first walk.

The app does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Why we process data (legal bases)

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies: we do not collect or receive personal data. The data described above is processed by the app on your own devices, at your request, to provide the features you use.

Retention

Data stays on your devices until you use Settings › Start fresh or delete the app. Start fresh on one device also clears the progress on your paired iPhone or Apple Watch the next time they exchange data. Deleting the app removes only the copy on that device. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include the app's data according to your backup settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Erase your progress with Settings › Start fresh; deleting the app also removes its data from that device.
  • Change or revoke Apple Health access at any time in the Health app or in your device's Settings. Revoking access stops new readings; to remove progress already stored, use Start fresh.
  • Turn notifications off in your device's Settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete, but you can write to codexxdev77@gmail.com with any question. See also Delete your data.

Children

WRISTPET is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, for example if a future version adds purchases, sync or any outside service, we will update this page before that version is released and mention it in the update notes.

Delete your data

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Data in the app

WRISTPET has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of or close. Your companion, progress and walk memories are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

  1. Open WRISTPET › Settings.
  2. Tap Start fresh and confirm.

Start fresh also clears the progress on your paired iPhone or Apple Watch the next time they exchange data. Deleting the app removes its data from that device only, so delete it on both devices to remove everything. Device backups you made in iCloud or on a computer are managed in your backup settings.

Apple Health

WRISTPET never writes to Apple Health, so deleting its data does not change your Health records. To stop the app reading steps, turn its access off in the Health app or in your device's Settings.
